# Anti-patterns: the incidents behind the hard rules

1. **LLM-fabricated facts.** Copy said "Argentina opens the World Cup at AT&T Stadium · June 11". Mexico vs South Africa opened at Estadio Azteca, and Argentina's first match was June 16 at Arrowhead (Kansas City). Six variants had been built on it, and 5 needed re-renders. Later runs caught "Canada won on home soil" (Los Angeles) and "Messi's 7th World Cup" (6th, 19 goals) before publishing. Rule: research comes before writing. The researcher hands over a facts table with a verdict per claim: `✅ VERIFIED` (2 or more independent sources: FIFA, Wikipedia, major outlets, venue sites), `⚠ UNVERIFIED` (one source) or `❗ DISPUTED` (sources disagree). Only VERIFIED facts go into a script.
2. **Cross-brand pollution.** Brand A's launch was queued to a dormant sibling brand's YouTube, where it would have trained that channel's algorithm on the wrong niche. It was deleted by group UUID. Rule: brand A goes only to brand A and personal channels. A channel with no post in 30+ days counts as dormant. Cross-promotion to an active brand needs an agreed cross-promo framing.
3. **Right of publicity.** A real player's face nearly went into a persona prompt. US state right-of-publicity law (statutory damages), ECHR Art. 8, GDPR Art. 9, athlete NIL contracts and platform ad policies all forbid unauthorised likenesses, and AI-generated ones count. Use fictional personas, synthetic voices, no logos, and crowds shot from a distance. For editorial use of real stars, use the CC-photo lane in SKILL.md.
4. **Ripped footage.** See SKILL.md "Rights and facts". It is the one mistake that kills a channel.
5. **Discord 25 MB.** A 33 MB 45-second video failed at publish with a generic error. Re-encode for Discord before scheduling.
6. **Silent `[]` without `showorg`.** 20 minutes were spent believing no channels were connected.
7. **Cloudflare 1010 on curl's default UA.** The API was reachable all along; the edge was blocking the request.
8. **`PUT /api/posts/{id}` 404 and `DELETE` returning `{"error":true}` on success.** Verify state with a separate GET rather than trusting response bodies.
