# Stock footage and audio

## Free for commercial use, no attribution

- **Pexels** (best overall; stadium mid-shots, crowds, travel, city aerials). API: `https://api.pexels.com/videos/search?query=...` with `Authorization: $PEXELS_API_KEY`.
- **Pixabay** (motion-graphic intros and outros, drone flyovers). API: `https://pixabay.com/api/videos/?key=$PIXABAY_API_KEY&q=...`.
- **Mixkit** (modern Reels and Shorts-style clips).
- **Coverr**, **Motion Places**, **Mazwai**: free; check each clip.
- **Videvo**, **Videezy**: often require attribution; check each clip.

Pond5, Getty, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock and Storyblocks are paid and rarely worth it for short-form. Only buy Getty/Imagn editorial stills for the real-player lane, and only non-match material.

## Editorial vs commercial

A clip marked "editorial only" (real venues with branding, player faces, team kits in a match) cannot be used in an ad, even after paying. Safe: generic stadium exteriors with no logos, crowds too far away to recognise anyone, field action with no kits or faces in focus. Unsafe: visible logos or kits, recognisable players or coaches, branded merch close-ups. When unsure, blur it or cover it with an overlay.

## Working conventions

- Save clips to `video/public/stock/<subject>-<source>-<id>.mp4`; the source suffix makes the licence traceable later.
- Filter by orientation: vertical for TikTok, Reels and Shorts; horizontal for LinkedIn and YouTube pre-roll.
- Never reuse a clip across videos (see SKILL.md).

## Ambience and SFX

Freesound (prefer CC0) and Pixabay Sound Effects. Search "soccer crowd", "stadium roar", "goal celebration".
