## Channel triage

Pick a deliberate subset of channels, never everything connected. For each one, check:

- Does its audience care about this launch? (A WC-26 launch on a baby-app channel is noise.)
- Is it ready to be associated with this brand? Dormant sibling brands are off-limits.
- Does the format fit? Vertical for short-form mobile; horizontal for LinkedIn and YouTube pre-roll.
- Which framing does it want? LinkedIn favours the founder story; TikTok favours the product; X can take either, kept tight.

## Copy by channel (WC-26 examples)

- **Personal X:** founder voice, the stack, concrete pricing. "Built an AI travel concierge for the FIFA World Cup 2026 in 14 days using Claude Code + Remotion. Free at wc-26.net. First 1,000 founders get Pro for $3.49/mo for life."
- **Personal IG / Threads:** first person, hashtags for discovery, link in bio rather than in the caption.
- **Personal LinkedIn:** lesson-led, a specific technical achievement, no emoji.
- **YouTube:** a title-style line; the video carries the rest.
- **Telegram:** lead with the news, no fluff.
- **Brand YouTube / IG / FB:** product framing, list of features, SEO hashtags. The IG version adds emoji and middot separators.
- **Brand Discord:** `@everyone` for launches only, and ask for feedback.

## Checklist

Caption length suits the platform; hashtag counts are X 1–2, IG 5–10, LinkedIn 3–5, FB 1–3, YT 3–5; link placement suits the platform (no caption links on IG); dimensions match the platform. With a global audience, stagger posts by the primary timezone.
